Privacy Policy — Tihoka

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

Tihoka stores your learning data on your device. If you enable sync, your data is written to your own Google Drive. We do not operate a server, and we never receive your data.

Data the extension stores

  • Saved words, decks, review history, and settings.
  • Stored locally in your browser profile by default.

Google Drive sync (optional)

When you connect a Google account, Tihoka requests these OAuth scopes:

  • drive.appdata — writes sync data to the hidden app-data folder in your Drive. Only Tihoka can see this folder.
  • drive.file — reads and writes files that Tihoka itself created in your Drive.
  • openid, email — identifies which account you connected.

Sync traffic goes directly between your browser and Google's Drive API. No third party — including us — can read it.

What we never do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No collection, sale, or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your Drive files that Tihoka did not create.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect your Google account in the extension settings at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local and Drive data remain yours to keep or delete.
