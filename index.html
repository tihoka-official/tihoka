Look up

Click a word — the answer appears in place

No new tab, no lost context. The dictionary popup opens right on the subtitle with definitions, sentence breakdown and translation — from dictionaries and from AI providers you pick.

  • Word meaning, sentence breakdown, and translation in one popup.
  • Powered by ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, or Claude — your choice per tab.

Customize

AI answers, on your terms

Every AI answer runs on a request template you own — one JSON document holding the rules, the data, and the answer schema. Plus HTML & CSS templates for how results look.

  • Rename output keys freely — the reader parses by your schema.
  • Layout answers with {{slots}}, data-each loops and data-check checkboxes.
  • Validate before saving — broken templates can't ship.

Remember

Tick what matters — it becomes a flashcard

Answers arrive with checkboxes. Tick the chunk, word, or translation worth keeping and it goes into your deck — nothing enters that you didn't choose.

  • Spaced-repetition decks inside Tihoka, or export to Anki.
  • Optional Google Drive sync — to a hidden folder on your own account.

From watching to remembering

The loop takes seconds per word — the review compound does the rest.

Watch with Tihoka subtitles

Open a supported streaming site. Tihoka overlays readable subtitles and tracks the sentence you're on.

Click any word you don't know

The dictionary popup shows dictionary entries plus AI answers — explanation, sentence breakdown, translation — from the provider you picked.

Tick what goes into your deck

Checkboxed answers feed straight into cards. Nothing enters your deck that you didn't tick.

Review in Anki

Export to Anki or review in built-in decks. Optional Google Drive sync keeps everything backed up under your account.

Your data stays yours

All learning data lives on your device. Sync is opt-in and writes only to a hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive.
