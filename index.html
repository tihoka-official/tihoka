Tihoka

A Chrome extension that turns streaming video into a language-learning session.

What it does

  • Downloads and displays subtitles on supported streaming sites.
  • Tap-to-translate dictionary lookups while you watch.
  • Saves words into spaced-repetition decks and exports Anki cards.
  • Optionally syncs your decks and settings to your own Google Drive.

Your data

All learning data stays on your device. Sync is opt-in and writes only to a hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive. See the Privacy Policy.
