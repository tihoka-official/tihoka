See how AI customization works How it works

Everything happens while you watch

No switching apps, no pausing to search. The learning layer sits on top of the video.

⌄

Subtitle display

Downloads and shows subtitles on supported streaming sites, styled to stay readable on any scene.

❖

Tap-to-translate

Click a word in the subtitle — a dictionary popup gives the meaning, breakdown, and sentence translation in place.

✦

AI answers, your rules

ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek or Claude answer inside the popup — and you can rewrite exactly what they're asked and how results look.

▣

Anki export

Tick the parts worth keeping and they go into flashcards — words, chunks, translations — ready for spaced repetition.

♻

Decks & sync

Saved words live in spaced-repetition decks. Optional sync writes to a hidden app-data folder on your own Google Drive.

◍

Private by default

All learning data stays on your device. Sync is opt-in. No accounts, no analytics on what you learn.

Your AI, your format

Every AI answer in the popup is driven by a request template you own — one JSON document. Change the instructions, rename the output keys, and Tihoka parses by the schema you declared.

  • One JSON per feature — request and answer contract in a single document.
  • output is the parse spec: pick your own key names, the reader follows.
  • HTML + CSS templates control how every answer looks.
  • data-check marks what can go into your Anki cards.

From watching to remembering

The loop takes seconds per word — the review compound does the rest.

Watch with Tihoka subtitles

Open a supported streaming site. Tihoka overlays readable subtitles and tracks the sentence you're on.

Click any word you don't know

The dictionary popup shows dictionary entries plus AI answers — explanation, sentence breakdown, translation — from the provider you picked.

Tick what goes into your deck

Checkboxed answers feed straight into cards. Nothing enters your deck that you didn't tick.

Review in Anki
