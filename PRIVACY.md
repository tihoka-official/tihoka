# Tihoka Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

## What Tihoka does

Tihoka is a language study tool. It looks up words, captures subtitles, and turns them into flashcards. Everything runs locally in your browser.

## What we collect

Nothing. Tihoka collects, transmits, sells, or shares no personal data or analytics. We operate no servers.

Your data — dictionaries, vocabulary, settings, captured subtitles — is stored locally on your device.

## Optional features that touch data outside your device

- **Google Drive sync**: if you enable it, your vocabulary and settings go directly from your browser to your own Google Drive account. Data never passes through our servers.
- **AnkiConnect**: card data is sent to AnkiConnect on your own machine (localhost:8765).

## Permissions

Tihoka asks for browser permissions only to provide its features — looking up words on the page you choose, detecting subtitles, saving files you request, and storing your data locally. These permissions are not used to collect or transmit your data.
